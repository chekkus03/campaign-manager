📖 Campaign Codex & Combat Rules

1. Battalion-to-Regiment Composition Rules

Every Regiment must contain exactly 3 Battalions. The combination determines the Regiment Type.

  • Infantry Regiment: Exactly 3 Infantry Battalions.
  • Motorized Regiment: Exactly 3 Infantry Battalions + all equipped with transport.
  • Mechanized Regiment: At least 2 Armored Infantry Battalions + 3rd slot (Tank, Recon, or Combat Engineer).
  • Armored Regiment: At least 2 Tank Battalions + 3rd slot (Armored Infantry, Tank Destroyer, or Recon).
  • Artillery Regiment: At least 2 Field Artillery Battalions + 3rd slot (Tank Destroyer, Field Artillery, or Combat Engineer).

2. Regiment-to-Division Composition Rules

Every Division must contain exactly 3 Regiments.

  • Infantry Division: 2 Infantry Regiments + 1 Artillery Regiment.
  • Motorized Division: 2 Motorized Regiments + 1 Artillery Regiment.
  • Mechanized Division: 2 Mechanized Regiments + 1 Artillery Regiment. *(Override: [1 Mech + 1 Inf + 1 Arty] defaults to Infantry Division)*
  • Armored Division: [1 Armored Regiment + 1 Mechanized Regiment + 1 Artillery Regiment] OR [2 Armored Regiments + 1 Artillery Regiment].
